# Final-E results draft

Under the fixed B25 allocation, learning the compound-supervised readout improved held-out-entity OR-boundary discrimination beyond compound-guided selection of an atomic-trained readout in both models (claim C1). On pooled bare OR TF/FT versus FF, excluding TT, Qwen's R_all, S_all and S_full AUROCs were 0.983280, 0.844643 and 0.860117; Llama's were 0.988610, 0.811593 and 0.822829. The original paired R_all − S_all gains were 0.138637 [0.118394, 0.155166] for Qwen and 0.177017 [0.149099, 0.201004] for Llama. Both lower bounds exceeded the separately declared .02 practical-gain threshold (C2). Values are metric-level means over samples 11/23/37; brackets denote pointwise conditional 95% intervals.

The fixed-five-topic macro gains were 0.151129 [0.125737, 0.172078] for Qwen and 0.157926 [0.122276, 0.186238] for Llama (C3). These support a separate equal-topic qualification, not a co-primary or a claim that every topic benefits. See [main table](tables/main.pdf) and [primary-gain figure](figures/primary-gain.pdf).

Both models met all four required retention checks against the frozen reduced-training canonical LR: atomic and AND AUROC, each pooled and macro (C4). The loss margins were .005 for atomic and .02 for AND. Qwen's atomic differences were -0.000104 [-0.000284, -0.000007] pooled and -0.000356 [-0.001213, -0.000002] macro (C5). These small negative differences remain losses even though they are within the declared margin. Retention does not establish zero loss, calibration or unchanged behavior. The [retention table](tables/retention.pdf) and [figure](figures/retention.pdf) show all eight checks.

The separate restored-full-training sensitivity, R_all − S_full, was 0.123162 [0.101639, 0.141084] pooled for Qwen and 0.165781 [0.140790, 0.187191] for Llama (C6); both lower bounds exceeded .02, and their separately reported macro intervals were also positive. This supports robustness to the tested full-training comparator without replacing the original primary. Exposure, preprocessing and balanced sampling change together, S_full still uses compound labels, and full-training repair was not tested.

S_full − S_atom_full had positive pooled and macro intervals in both models, supporting its separate comparison of compound-guided versus atomic-guided selection in the full bank (C7). R_all − S_atom_full was also positive at both scopes in both models (C8). These comparisons appear in the [secondary table](tables/secondary.pdf) and have different roles from the primary. D-selected bank and clean-compound references remain descriptive (C9).

The secondary results constrain broader interpretations. Across the six frozen R_all heads, both OR wording variants had negative pooled OR-boundary differences relative to bare wording; eleven of the twelve paired intervals were wholly negative, while Llama sample 37 for at-least-one wording crossed zero (C10). The [wording table](tables/wording.pdf) retains every sample rather than averaging interval endpoints. Constituent transfer also had unfavorable findings: under explicit-or-both wording, matched-layer OR-trained heads exceeded AND-trained transfer on several constituent endpoints by more than the frozen .05 margin (C11). The [constituent table](tables/constituent.pdf) shows all six pooled endpoints for both models, including uncertain comparisons. The [complete secondary inventory](secondary-results.md) retains control, sensitivity, topic, order and wording coverage, including point-below-.90 and unresolved classifications. Counts of such dependent rows are not independent findings.

Taken together, the original positive gain and separate retention checks support the locked benchmark improvement-with-retention claim in both models (C12). Practical magnitude, equal-topic weighting and restored-full-training robustness each rely on their own checks. This conjunction has no simultaneous or joint 95% coverage and concerns held-out entities within the benchmark, not unseen topics, calibration, behavioral truthfulness or causal use.
