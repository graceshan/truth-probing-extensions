# Final-E methods draft

We ask whether, under a fixed allocation of compound supervision, learning a linear readout improves discrimination of held-out-entity disjunctions beyond using compound labels to select an atomic-trained readout. The original comparison is R_all − S_all. Atomic and conjunction retention are separate requirements; restored-full-training sensitivity and equal-topic performance have their own evidential roles. The scientific procedures and interpretation were frozen before E evaluation. This paper package formats the accepted results and introduces no new fitting, selection, endpoints or inference.

We use **Qwen/Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct**, revision `a09a35458c702b33eeacc393d103063234e8bc28` (28 transformer blocks; width 3,584), and **meta-llama/Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct**, revision `0e9e39f249a16976918f6564b8830bc894c89659` (32 blocks; width 4,096). The frozen raw contract uses statement text without a chat template, tokenizer special tokens enabled, and the last nonpadding input token. Computation used BF16 and stored features used FP16, with batch size one, no truncation and unpadded inputs. Saved layers index Hugging Face `hidden_states[1:]` from zero, excluding the embedding output; the final saved layer is after the final RMSNorm. Model identifiers, revisions and representation details are from the [frozen raw contract](../../config/checkpoint_r2/full_raw_v1.json), whose hash is verified through the locked preparation configuration. These are the Final-E Qwen and Llama models, distinct from the earlier Qwen3 experiment in the historical repository README.

The benchmark spans cities, Spanish–English translations, inventors, element symbols and animal classes. Atomic statements include affirmative and negated variants. The reduced atomic fitting allocation contains 2,778 rows, with the prescribed 700-row balanced exposure recipe for bank methods requiring that subset; atomic development contains 1,012 rows. The compound reservation A15 contains 75 entities, 15 per topic. For each sample seed (11, 23, 37), five disjoint pairs per topic give 25 structured pair blocks. Each block expands over two operators, four truth cells (TT, TF, FT, FF) and both surface orders (AB, BA), yielding 400 dependent compound rows per sample. These are structured, correlated observations, not 400 independent binary labels. Substantial atomic supervision is part of the protocol. The frozen [protocol](../../config/checkpoint_r2/protocol_v1.json) specifies allocation, preprocessing, objectives, bank and selection rules; no minimum sufficient budget or sample-efficiency claim follows from this allocation.

The atomic-trained bank contains five L2 logistic-regression C values (.001, .01, .1, 1, 10) plus DoM, covariance-MM, t_G, TTPD and R0 at each saved layer: 280 candidates for Qwen and 320 for Llama. Reduced-training preprocessing uses coordinate means and population standard deviations from P only, a 1e-6 standard-deviation floor and zero masking of constant coordinates. **S_all** selects an atomic-trained candidate using pooled allocated-compound OR TF/FT-versus-FF AUROC. The frozen eligibility criterion requires atomic D AUROC within .005 of the reduced canonical LR. The canonical saved layers are 17 (Qwen, LR C=1) and 15 (Llama, LR C=10). **S_atom_all** uses atomic D ranking within its atomic eligibility gate and never uses compound labels for its ranking.

**R_all** learns one linear head per layer with objective `0.5 mean atomic BCE + 0.5 mean allocated-compound BCE + 0.001 ||w||²`, with an unpenalized intercept. The same head scores AND and OR; it receives no separate operator indicator, constituent score or parse feature. The compound block weights pairs, operators, cells and orders equally. Layer selection uses the mean of five entity-disjoint fold OR-boundary AUROCs, with one pair per topic held out in each fold; scores from different fitted heads are not pooled. The selected layer is refitted on all 25 allocated pairs. Atomic-retention eligibility and the frozen refit/fallback rules remain in force. Ties use the specified atomic D criterion, continuous standardized OR separation, and freshly seeded PCG64 choice among sorted tied identifiers at tolerance 1e-12. The frozen objective specification is also documented [here](../../docs/selection_repair_objectives.md). The registered R_all saved layers for samples 11/23/37 are Qwen 20/22/18 and Llama 14/13/13; the [registry](../../config/final_e/head_registry_v1.json) binds the exact fitted artifacts. No head, layer, seed, threshold or preprocessing was selected using E.

**S_full** restores the full 3,040-row atomic training exposure and its corresponding preprocessing and 1,000-row balanced exposure recipe, retaining the finite atomic bank and compound-guided selection. **S_atom_full** selects in that full bank using atomic labels. The [full-bank configuration](../../config/checkpoint_next/full_bank_prep_v1.json) and frozen registry define these comparators. R_all − S_full tests the separately frozen full-training sensitivity; S_full − S_atom_full tests its own selection-label-benefit comparison; R_all − S_atom_full is an additional preserved comparison. Exposure, preprocessing and balanced sampling change together. S_full still uses compound labels, and full-training repair was not tested. D-selected bank and C_clean heads are fixed descriptive references, not E-selected optima. The [contrast manifest](../../config/final_e/contrasts_v1.json) preserves all controls and sensitivity comparisons.

E uses reviewed, score-independent fact eligibility and the frozen degree-four within-topic pair construction. It comprises 450 canonical pairs among 225 compound entities and 7,200 bare compound rows (3,600 per operator); the OR boundary excludes TT, leaving 2,700 rows. There are 974 admitted atomic rows in a separate 294-entity frame. Compound entity counts are animals 16, cities 146, elements 18, inventors 11 and Spanish 34. The [E manifest](../../data/clean_protocol/compounds/entity_disjoint/final_e_preparation_v2/manifest.json) records topic-specific counts and conditions. These held-out entities were excluded from readout fitting and selection, but need not be unfamiliar to the pretrained models. E does not test unseen topics. All four cells and both surface orders share entities and pairs, so row counts are not independent sample sizes.

The original primary is **pooled bare OR TF/FT-versus-FF AUROC, R_all minus S_all, separately for each model**, excluding TT. The source report supplies the ordinary mean of the three per-sample metrics and paired differences, both for the observed point and within every bootstrap draw. We do not average fitted-head scores, concatenate cross-head predictions, average interval endpoints or recompute bootstrap intervals in this package. A positive primary interval lower bound supports gain; a lower bound strictly above .02 separately supports practical gain. A positive fixed-five-topic macro lower bound supports the equal-topic qualification. Macro is not co-primary and does not establish improvement within every topic.

Retention compares R_all with the **frozen reduced-training canonical LR evaluated on E**, for atomic AUROC and bare AND AUROC, each at pooled and macro scopes. Loss margins are .005 and .02, respectively. Retention is supported when the paired lower bound is at least the negative margin; an upper bound strictly below the boundary supports loss beyond the margin. Intervals crossing the boundary or undefined intervals are unresolved. A point estimate alone cannot substitute for interval support. Retention is about ranking discrimination, not calibration, decisions or behavior.

The frozen inference uses 2,000 topic-stratified entity-bootstrap draws from PCG64(1729), with separate atomic and compound frames. Within each frame, models, methods, samples, wordings and comparisons share draws. Atomic row weights are entity multiplicities; compound weights are products of endpoint multiplicities, shared across dependent cells and surface orders. Macro uses an ordinary mean of all five fixed topics. Intervals are 95% pointwise percentile intervals with linear quantiles and at least 1,800 finite draws. Undefined draws are retained without redrawing, imputation or dropping topics. Intervals are conditional on frozen fits, selectors and evaluated support. Multiple passing checks do not imply simultaneous or joint atomic–compound 95% coverage. These rules are authoritative in the [final scientific lock](../../config/final_e/final_lock_20261004.json).

Frozen secondary conditions include “or both,” “at least one” and “both of the following” wording variants, excluded from fitting and selection; changing one operator reuses the bare counterpart for the other. Constituent probes decode the truth of FIRST and SECOND surface constituents, with targets remapped for AB/BA order. Source-only layer selection ranks the minimum, then mean, of six equal-topic marginal/conditional AUROCs; cross-operator comparisons use matched-layer target-trained references. Frozen composition uses min logits for AND and max logits for OR, and Boolean combinations at probability ≥ .5. The report retains marginal/conditional AUROC, accuracy, balanced accuracy, joint correctness and composition endpoints. The .90 absolute target and .05 transfer margin have their existing classifications; constituent transfer deficits are target-trained minus source-trained on the target operator. The [secondary inventory](secondary-results.md) links all conditions, controls and unfavorable findings.
