Decompose global ranking across and within topics
For each model, sample and OR wording, form a five by five matrix. Entry i,j is AUROC comparing mixed TF/FT positives from topic i with FF negatives from topic j, using the same head’s scores. Report the bare matrix, wording matrix and paired difference. Diagonal cells are within-topic discrimination; off-diagonal cells expose cross-topic comparability.
Reconstruct pooled AUROC exactly as the weighted sum of matrix cells, with weight n_positive_i × n_negative_j divided by total positives × total negatives, counting ties as one half. Report diagonal and off-diagonal contributions and their masses. Equal-topic macro AUROC is the unweighted diagonal mean; it is not the within-topic contribution to pooled AUROC. Reproduce the published pooled and macro values before interpreting the decomposition.
Compute each sample separately, then average metrics or matrix entries; never concatenate different heads’ scores. Reuse paired entity draws and recalculate cell weights within each draw. A zero-mass cell has undefined cell AUROC but contributes zero to pooled AUROC. If total positive or negative mass is zero, pooled AUROC is undefined. An undefined diagonal makes the fixed five-topic macro undefined; do not average only surviving topics. Record undefined counts without redrawing. The 25 cells are dependent descriptive diagnostics.
