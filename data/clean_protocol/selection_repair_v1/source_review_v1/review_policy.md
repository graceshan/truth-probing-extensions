# T2B review semantics (recorded before adjudication)

Reviewer: Codex-assisted external review, not human verification.
Scope: exactly the 30 frozen T2A queue rows in draw order; source facts across
train/D/E only. Franklin/France and institutional identity corroborations are
separate non-sample reviews. T2A remains provisional.

Residence means an established home, ordinary place of living, or documented
residential study/employment posting. Birthplace or nationality alone is not
residence evidence. Travel, conferences, touring, demonstrations, holidays and
short visits alone do not establish residence. No invented duration cutoff
resolves ambiguous temporary stays: mark unresolved when sources do not establish
the nature of the stay. Residence can occur more than once and in many countries.

For supported_true require explicit residence or a clearly residential posting
in the stated country. For supported_false require an adequately detailed life/
residence chronology accounting for major life phases, with a reasoned explanation
of why the proposed country conflicts with that history. This is defeasible
historical evidence, not proof of universal nonresidence. Nationality, search
snippets, or omission from a short biography alone cannot support false.
Insufficient chronology, inaccessible relevant evidence, conflicting sources or
ambiguous subject/geography must produce unresolved.

Use T2A country spelling mappings without changing historical data. Do not equate
historical polities with present-day borders or silently collapse constituent
nations. If the relevant location changes sovereignty or historical/current
geography changes the judgment and the original semantics do not resolve it,
mark unresolved. Slash-separated countries are source lists with no specified
AND/OR truth semantics: no automatic truth judgment from one component; record
component evidence and unresolved if the exact whole claim is ambiguous.
For joint-person subjects, inspect both people. Evidence about only one cannot
establish that both lived there. If distributive/collective readings differ,
record unresolved rather than choose a convenient reading.

Every row receives supported_false, supported_true or unresolved, with inspected
source text, URL/title/retrieval date/locator, reasoning and limitations. Failed
fetches and search snippets provide no evidence. Keep all 30 in the denominator,
including errors and unresolved cases. No replacement, redraw or sample editing.
Recommend paired exclusion for supported_true or unresolved; do not apply it or
relabel sources here. Any targeted follow-up rule is a proposal requiring review.

Report all three outcome counts. Claim zero detected errors only if all 30 are
resolved and none is supported_true. Even 0/30 would not certify remaining
negatives; the conservative binomial one-sided 95% upper bound is about 9.5%.
This bound is a context statement, not an estimate treating unresolved as correct.
